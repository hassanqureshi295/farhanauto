Overview

What This Service Covers

Modern vehicles run dozens of interconnected electrical circuits — from the starter and charging system to sensors, lighting, and infotainment. When one circuit fails, it rarely shows up as a single obvious symptom.

We start every job with a full circuit trace using calibrated diagnostic equipment, not guesswork. Once the fault is isolated, we repair the wiring, connector, or component directly — reinsulated, tested under load, and verified before your car leaves the bay.

This applies to intermittent faults too: flickering lights, dead sockets, dashboard warnings that come and go, or a battery that won't hold charge.

Recognize The Problem

Signs You Need This Service

If you're noticing any of the below, it's worth getting checked before it strands you.

💡

Flickering Dashboard Lights

Warning icons or gauges that flicker, dim, or reset randomly while driving.

🔋

Battery Drains Overnight

Car struggles to start in the morning despite a battery that tested fine before.

⚡

Dead Power Outlets or Sockets

USB ports, cigarette lighter, or power windows stop responding intermittently.

🔧

Burning Smell Near Dashboard

A faint electrical or burning odor, especially after extended use.

🚨

Check Engine Light On

Persistent check engine or battery warning light with no clear cause.

🎵

Static in Audio or Sensors

Interference in the stereo, parking sensors, or camera feed while the engine runs.

Our Process

How We Diagnose & Repair

The same four-step sequence for every electrical job, so nothing gets guessed at.

01

Circuit Trace & Scan

We connect diagnostic equipment and manually trace the affected circuit to pinpoint the exact fault location.

02

Confirm the Root Cause

We isolate whether it's a wiring break, corroded connector, faulty relay, or component failure — and show you.

03

Repair & Reinsulate

Faulty sections are repaired to OEM standard, reinsulated, and secured so the fix holds long-term.

04

Load Test & Verify

We test the full circuit under real load and re-scan before handing the car back to you.
